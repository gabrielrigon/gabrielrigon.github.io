Track record

Intern in 2010, then junior developer, tech lead, and for the last eight years software architect. Today I'm Head of Technology at Appolus Tecnologia.

That path made me a full-range engineer, from HTML and CSS to embedded software, with a lot of flight hours in infrastructure between the two. I've built institutional websites, a childcare app in Europe, SAP integrations, APIs for Brazil's national health data network, high-performance search over large medical bases, and systems with 1,200+ tables and 35,000 people logged in daily.

I've always worked inside the problem, not next to it, through audits, outages, migrations and public deadlines that don't move. I'm still here because building something a couple of million people actually use never stopped being a good reason to get up.

On my own time I run GBRLRG, a small studio where I make My Plan Buddy, Tablewit and Jambu, plus Mindminer, where I teach AI in Portuguese.

Away from all that, I'm into good food, the beach, trekking, travel and sun.

How I work

Simple solutions to complex problems. The architecture is whatever serves the outcome best, and I care about the result, the quality, and not wasting the team's time.

I lead teams of 2 to 20 with the same technical depth, and I'm still close to the code, because you can't hold a technical conversation you can't have yourself. I understand the problem, cut it to its essentials, ship it and measure what happened.

I also build for handover from day one, writing decisions down, naming owners, and setting standards a team can hold without me in the room. If the work only works with me in it, it isn't finished.
